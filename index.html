WidgetVerse Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

WidgetVerse ("the app") is a home screen widget app for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what happens to it. In short: the app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and your personal information stays on your phone. The only thing the app sends over the internet is a request for the weather forecast.

Information the app uses

Every item below is optional. The app asks for permission only when you choose a widget that needs it, and works without it.

Health Connect

If you connect Health Connect, WidgetVerse reads only your step count, only to display it on your widgets. If you allow background access, step widgets keep updating while the app is closed. Data from Health Connect is not transferred off your device, shared with anyone, used for advertising or used to build profiles, and is handled in line with the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can remove access at any time in Health Connect.

Weather service

Forecasts come from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) at api.met.no. Each request contains your chosen city's position, or your approximate location rounded to about 1 km, and identifies the app. MET Norway's own terms and privacy statement apply to those requests. Weather data is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No background location: location is used only while the app is open, then saved on your phone.

Backups and deleting your data

Your widget settings may be included in your phone's own Android backup if you have it turned on. Everything the app keeps is on your phone: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it. You can also withdraw any permission at any time in Android settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
